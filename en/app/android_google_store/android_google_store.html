Android app distribution policy

Current status and future regarding distribution on Google Play Store.

explanation

Currently, the app I developed is not distributed on Google Play Store. One reason is that continuous quality assurance is difficult for individual developers. The other thing is that the Google Play Store has strict reviews. With recent requirements, maintaining the evaluation period and the number of people evaluating is a very high hurdle for individuals. Additionally, the Google Play Store is scheduled to begin reviewing app updates starting in September 2026, and distribution is expected to become even more difficult in the future. Therefore, I have given up on publishing it on the Google Play Store and am considering distributing it on F-Droid instead. F-Droid is an open source application store for Android devices.

F-Droid only offers open source apps, unlike Google Play Store. This allows users to use apps with privacy and security in mind.can be used. We continued to verify the latest conditions, but it turned out that it could not be installed even on F-Droid. For this reason, we have now given up on distributing it on F-Droid and have decided to only publish the source code on GitHub. We apologize for the inconvenience, but if you are interested, please check the source code on GitHub.
