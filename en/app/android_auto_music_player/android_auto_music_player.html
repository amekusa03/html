Android Auto Music Player

This is a local music player for Android developed to reuse old smartphones as music players.

About this Project

It was developed with the aim of reusing a smartphone that is no longer in use as a music player. You can play MP3 files by specifying a folder, and it also supports Android Auto.

Almost all processes from design to code generation to debuggingClaude CodeWe proceeded through dialogue with AI also takes the lead in deep root cause investigations and fixes, such as issues with Android Auto's artwork display and shuffle button. The generated code is reviewed by humans and its operation is confirmed on actual machines.

Video

Development Environment

  • Kotlin
  • Kotlin Coroutines
  • AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer)
  • Media3 MediaLibraryService
  • androidx.car.app (Android Auto)
  • Android SDK (API 26+)

Key Features

  • Folder selection playback: You can play MP3 files at once by specifying a folder.
  • Album art display: Displays the album art embedded in the song on the screen.
  • Shuffle Playback: You can play songs in random order.
  • Search function: You can narrow down your search by song title, artist name, or album name.
  • Background play: It continues playing in the foreground service even if the app is closed.
  • Android Auto compatible: You can control the songs from the in-vehicle display.
  • Caching scan results: Keeps startup quick and updates automatically when changing folders.

Known Issues / Out of Scope

  • Google Assistant voice control (“Call 〇〇”): Although the intent filter addition and reception processing have been implemented, it is up to the OS to decide which app the assistant will delegate playback to. Substantive verification has not been possible as YouTube Music and Spotify may take priority and we cannot guarantee that users will be directed to this app.

Setup

Please follow the steps below to set up your project.

  1. Download and install Android Studio.
  2. Clone this repository.
  3. Open the project in Android Studio.
  4. Install the required dependencies.
