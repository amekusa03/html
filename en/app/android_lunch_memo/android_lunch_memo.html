Lunch Memo

Lunch Memo is an Android app that allows you to easily manage your lunch schedule. Make your daily lunch more comfortable with Material 3 design and notification features.

About this Project

Lunch Memo is an Android app that lets you manage your daily lunch plans and reservations in style. We have devised a way to quickly leave notes using calendar-style card operations. We will notify you of today's schedule via a noon notification.

Video

Key Features

  • Smart records: Swipe through the calendar-style cards to quickly jot down what you've had for lunch.
  • Lunch notification: Every day at 12:00, you will be notified of that day's lunch memo.
  • Modern design: It takes full advantage of Material 3 and features smooth card animations and gradient backgrounds.
  • Notification permission support: Compatible with notification permissions for Android 13 and above.
  • Clean data retention: Automatically organize past notes to keep the app nimble.

Technology Stack

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose (Material 3)
  • Room (SQLite)
  • WorkManager
  • Coroutines & Flow
  • MVVM
  • Gradle Version Catalog (libs.versions.toml)
  • KSP (Kotlin Symbol Processing)

Setup

  1. Open your project in Android Studio (recommended for Ladybug or newer).
  2. Run Gradle Sync.
  3. Run the app on your Android device or emulator.
