LocTime

Android app that delivers notifications tailored to location, language, and time

About this Project

"LocTime" is an app that sends notifications according to a specific location and time. You can flexibly set notifications to suit your lifestyle, such as notifying you of break times at work or work hours a few minutes in advance. We envision situations where you don't need time notifications when you're at home, but you want time notifications when you're at work. You can receive time-of-day notifications tailored to specific locations and times.
The name "LocTime" means that it enables notifications tailored to various locations and times.

Development Environment

  • Jetpack Compose

Key Features

  • Location and time notification: Display notifications tailored to specific locations and times.
  • Specify location by address: You can specify a more precise location by entering an address.
  • User friendly: Easy to operate with intuitive UI.
