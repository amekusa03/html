About this Journey

In order to improve the music listening experience on a PC, we record the changes in the audio environment that have been through trial and error for more than 10 years. This is a process that pursues sound resolution and noise countermeasures, such as transitioning from analog connections to digital output, and making or replacing speakers yourself.

Step 6.2

Power is supplied from the PC power supply to the amplifier.
TSS-1 - The AC adapter was broken, so it was modified to supply power from the PC power supply.
Originally, the power supply for TSS-1 is DC15V, but preparing a power supply just for this is overspec for regular use. As a last resort, I modified it to supply power from the PCIe power connector of the PC power supply.
This allows the amplifier to start up at the same time as the PC is turned on, improving convenience.
However, since it is powered from the 12V line of the PC power supply, a voltage lower than the rated voltage of TSS-1 will be supplied. It's unclear how this will affect the amp, but it's currently working fine.
Modifications to the power supply are carried out at your own risk and caution should be taken as there is a risk of damage to the equipment due to incorrect connections or overvoltages.
