# 🛰 Comparison of satellite communication service companies and the future of Rakuten Mobile

2026-09-07

## Introduction

Since Rakuten Mobile is expected to start satellite communication services soon, we compared and considered the current situation with other major carriers (au, Docomo, Softbank).

Currently, when comparing the availability of each company, Rakuten Mobile seems to be a little behind the curve. Additionally, the usage fee for satellite communication has not been announced at this time, so if it becomes a paid option, it will likely be a big disadvantage.

## 🎯Summary and future choices

Currently, I am using Rakuten Mobile as my main line. The monthly data amount is less than 3GB and the Rakuten Link calls are about 10 times a month, so the monthly fee is around 1,078 yen (980 yen excluding tax).

I have subscribed to povo as a sub-line as insurance against occasional out-of-service areas and over 3GB, but so far I have not experienced any situation where I actually felt that having a sub-line would have helped me. Due to low maintenance costs, it remains in a salty state.

Based on this comparison and consideration:

- **If Rakuten Mobile's satellite communication is provided free of charge (within the basic charge)**:
  Continue to use Rakuten Mobile. Since concerns about being out of service area will be greatly reduced, canceling the sub-line (POVO) will also be considered.
- **If it is a paid option**:
  I would like to consider switching to another company's plan or canceling Rakuten Mobile itself.
