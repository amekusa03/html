## background

When you get stuck in a swamp, you want to do various things and make many mistakes.
In this case, making it possible to manage settings with a GUI app will reduce mistakes.
In line with recent trends, we decided to create the GUI using AI.
Creating a PyQt5 GUI has become really easy.

![GUI](essays/2026-08-20-pipewire.png)

## overview

1. Clock & sampling rate settings
2. Buffer size (Quantum) & resample settings
3. One-click preset function
    Presets for each purpose are available that do not require manual settings.
4. Real-time playback monitoring & logs
5. Edit configuration file directly

## evaluation

Change the rate, change the Quantum size, and check the changes due to sound source and DSP.

I don't play PC games, but I mainly play CD sound sources converted to MP3 on Rhythmbox, and I feel very satisfied when they are played at 44100Hz on Rhythmbox.
