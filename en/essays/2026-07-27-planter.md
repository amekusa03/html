# A story about growing flowers with an air conditioner

## 2026-07-27

## I forget

When you suddenly realize that you forgot to water the flowers on your balcony.
Be careful, it's not easy.

## There

There was a water source on the balcony.
It's the air conditioner's drain hose.
Water was dripping from the drain hose. Just use this.

## DIY

However, the drain hose is short and cannot reach the planter where flowers are planted.
When I went to my local home improvement store, they sold drain hoses for air conditioners by the meter.
Simply connect this to the air conditioner's drain hose and extend it.
However, if you place the planter in a high place, you won't be able to reach it, so you need to place it in a low place.

When I tried DIY, water arrived in the planter exactly as I had calculated (?).
You can do this!

## improvement

After a few days, the amount of water becomes a bit large and there is a puddle inside the planter...
This can't be done. Plants need as much water as they need, and not too much.
Improved here. I removed the soil from the planter and placed pumice stones at the bottom to improve drainage.
This is resolved.

## Be careful

Since the water from air conditioners is not clean, it would be best to avoid growing vegetables etc.
Also, since there is only soil at the top of the planter, only small flowers can be planted.

## Now

Even the clumsy and lazy person like me can now enjoy balcony gardening.
