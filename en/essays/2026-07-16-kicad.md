## Easy to use

### Japanese

It's all in solid Japanese.
With this, you can safely make detailed settings.

### The beginning

As is often the case, it is difficult to know where to start.
With this, you can place the first part just by clicking on the icon and placing it.
If in doubt, select the part you want from "Placement" in the menu.

### wiring

I only use the circuit diagram, but I was surprised at how many parts there were.
If you were a little careful with the placement, you could draw the circuit diagram in no time.

### Version management

It also supports Git.
There is no need to use a separate tool, and there seems to be update monitoring as well.
