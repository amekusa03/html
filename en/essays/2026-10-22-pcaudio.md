## Review

The sound quality is now good enough for listening while sitting at my PC.
In an apartment building, this is probably as loud as I can go. Anything louder would just be noise.
I never thought the day would come when I’d start by replacing the motherboard connectors and end up swapping out the speaker cables.
I’m not completely satisfied with every aspect of the sound, but I’ve reached my goal for now. I think.
With jazz, I can hear the subtle nuances of the piano’s touch, and with pop, I can even sense the breath of the vocalist.
I’m perfectly satisfied with this. …Or so I’d like to think.

What struck me here was that audio design follows the same basic principles as analog circuitry—there’s nothing special about it.
It’s about minimizing signal loss and reducing noise. By repeating this process, sound quality improves.
I’d like to believe this is the result of simply sticking to the basics without overcomplicating things.

Let's enjoy music.
