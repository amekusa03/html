# Music files are stuck in the swamp

## 2026-01-07

I've been organizing my files little by little for many years now. One of the most troublesome things is music files. It has already taken many years to get to this point.

Some of the sound sources were moved from analog to digital. Divide files by song and add song information using CDDB. When I decided to listen to it, I noticed the noise that is typical of records. However, I don't have time to work on noise removal. Of course, there are also sound sources that were originally CDs.

## I can't listen to the song I want to listen to

Now, a problem arises. I can't listen to the songs I want to listen to.

What was the title of that song? What was that artist's name? I don't want to blame it on my age, but even though ``Satchmo'' appears, ``Louis Armstrong'' just doesn't appear.

Even more troublesome, after listening to Bill Evans, Morning Musume. (Although Mo Musume is also an important presence).

I tried making a playlist for commuting, but after about a month I got tired of it and had to start selecting songs from a huge collection of files again. This makes it impossible to enjoy music purely.

## The quagmire of genre classification

In order to overcome this problem, I started to decide on the genre based on the artist's name. I tried my best night after night, but in the end it didn't work.

Are the Beatles rock or pop? Is it okay to decide that Queen is rock music? What will happen to Michael Jackson? The more I think about it, the more I can't find an answer.
