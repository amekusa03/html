# The Car Audio Rabbit Hole: Your First Step Starts with the Head Unit

Stock-01-dd

## First, “What should we do about the head unit?”

When it comes to upgrading your car audio system, this first step is the most important of all.
I’ve owned and driven cars for decades now, and come to think of it, there’s never been a time when I’ve kept the audio system in its original condition.

My memory is a bit hazy, but I think it all started when I replaced the cassette deck in the first car I ever owned with a CD deck. Part of the reason was that CD decks were relatively affordable back then, and even though my memory of exactly how it sounded has faded, the sound quality was definitely an improvement.

## A Problem Unique to Modern Cars

However, in today’s cars, the head unit is often integrated with the display, and they come with connectivity services unique to factory-installed systems. You can’t just casually swap it out for an aftermarket unit like you used to, and if you do decide to replace it, it involves a fair amount of effort and expense.

That’s why choosing the right head unit—the starting point—is so important. While factory-installed units often have modest basic audio performance, some aftermarket models come equipped with a full-fledged DSP (digital signal processor) right out of the box.

## The Conventional Approach or “Experimental DIY”?

If you listen to music in your car often, choosing a decent head unit from the start is definitely the quickest way to go—and it’s undoubtedly the best approach.
