# Shizuoka Tourism Chubu

2026-07-21 / Daily life

## friend trip

Although it's rare, friends from outside the prefecture come to Shizuoka,
I was once asked to "show me around Shizuoka."
I would like to introduce some of the places that I have visited so far that seem to be good.
It's just a place I like,
Some people may be like, ``Eh, here?'' but I hope you don't mind.

## place

### A place where Mt. Fuji is beautiful

* Nihondaira Yume Terrace
* Miho no Matsubara
* Jurigi (it seems like a bear has appeared recently)

### vehicle

* Oigawa Railway (SL, Thomas etc.)
* Shimizu Port Marine Bus

### sightseeing

* Wasabi Factory (Lots of souvenirs here)
* Shimizu Port Marine Bus
* Yaizu fishing port (around the fish center)
* Sauna Shikiji
* Riverside City (Shimizu Ward)
* Sunpu Castle Park (a garden was built a few years ago)
* Horai Bridge (Shimada)
* Okure Beach (surprisingly popular)
* Kunozan Toshogu Shrine (requires a bit of physical strength)
* Umegashima Onsen (a little far)
* Sengen Shrine (Fujinomiya is the head office. There is also Shizuoka Sengen Shrine)
* Sumata Gorge Suspension Bridge

## food

* Oden (Shizuoka City Asama)
* Tororo (pronounced as Maruko)
* black hanpen
* Fujinomiya Yakisoba (Fujinomiya)
