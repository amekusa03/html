# Rakuten AI PPTX

## overview

I've been experimenting with AI from various companies, but today I was a little surprised by Rakuten AI's PPTX creation.
When creating presentation materials with AI, even the text may become images.
Often not editable.

Rakuten AI
・The image is difficult to edit, but if you ask for additional editing, it will be changed.
・Text and layout can be edited
・And **If you also export HTML, it will become one file including images**

Yes, it's surprising that it's just this one file.
Looking at the contents, it is difficult to edit manually, but
This is easier to handle when managing sources.

Formidable, Rakuten AI.
