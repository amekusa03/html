## Definition: Soft PLC

**Software PLC: **A control system in which the control logic is separated from dedicated hardware and can be executed on general-purpose hardware or a virtualized environment (hypervisor, etc.). 

On the other hand, products such as the Mitsubishi Electric Q series and OMRON NJ have unique specifications for both hardware and software, and these are called **hardware PLC**.

## What is CODESYS?

This software is compliant with IEC 61131-3 and allows you to integrate and develop PLC/Motion/HMI.
It has been used by over 400 OEM customers and thousands of end users around the world. 

* **Integrated development environment: **PLC, Motion, and HMI settings can be performed all-in-one. 
* **Asset reuse: **High portability of software assets due to compliance with international standards. 
* **Free use: **The development environment is available free of charge to users all over the world.

## Programming Languages

Programming is possible using major languages ​​defined by IEC 61131-3. 

* LD (Ladder Diagram)
* FBD (Function Block Diagram)
* ST (Structured Text)
* IL (Instruction List)
