# Unwanted photo deletion app

2026-08-26

## chance

One day, I was analyzing noise in music files. Suddenly, I decided to try my hand at organizing my photos. When I look at my camera roll, I notice a lot of out-of-focus photos. I wondered if this could be played automatically.

## Out of focus detection

Out of focus means that the edges are not clear. If so, it should be possible to detect it through image analysis. After trying out some libraries, I was able to implement this more easily than I expected.

## Organizing continuous shots

The trouble was taking continuous photos. There are dozens of photos with similar compositions. I tried to judge the superiority or inferiority by the content of the image, but it didn't work very well. After much deliberation, I decided to abandon my obsession with the image itself and switch to making judgments based on the date and time the photo was taken. This worked surprisingly well.

## The challenge of similar images

The most difficult part was detecting similar images. I solved it with an algorithm that uses hash values, but I ended up using the sample as a reference without fully understanding it. It's moving. However, I still feel that it was completed without making any sense.

## Difficulty in combining

The individual logics somehow took shape. However, combining these was not an easy task. Each interferes with the other, resulting in unexpected behavior. Through repeated trial and error, I created a shape that was easy for me to use.

## conclusion

![Similar search](essays/2026-08-26-photo.png)

All functions are complete. However, organizing years worth of photos still requires visual inspection, which takes a lot of effort. There are still many areas where apps can take over. I want to continue improving little by little.
