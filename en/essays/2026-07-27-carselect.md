# Purchase a new car with a budget of 2.5 million yen or less

## 2026-07-27

In the current automobile market, ``upper-grade light vehicles'' or ``base to intermediate grades of compact cars and small SUVs'' are realistic and extremely satisfying choices.

We will introduce standard car models often selected by general families, commuters, and people who use them for daily shopping, and their characteristics by body type.

## 1. Emphasis on family and practicality (sliding door car)

This is the most popular type for people transporting children and carrying a lot of luggage while shopping.

* Toyota Sienta (compact minivan):
  * Features: This is one of the few "regular car sliding doors and 3-row seats" model that can be purchased for less than 2.5 million yen. In addition to gasoline cars (starting at about 2,070,000 yen), depending on the grade, hybrid cars (starting at about 2,430,000 yen) also fit within your budget.
  * Advantages: The 5-number size makes it easy to drive, and it balances ease of everyday use with a sense of security when riding with a large number of people.
* Honda N-BOX / Suzuki Spacia (light super height wagon):
  * Features: This is a nationally popular car that boasts an overwhelming interior space that exceeds that of a light car and a high resale value. If you have a budget of 2.5 million yen, you can buy a new car with plenty of options, such as an upper grade with a turbo or a custom specification.
  * Advantages: You can use it as comfortably as a minivan while keeping maintenance costs such as vehicle tax and insurance costs low.

## 2. Emphasis on cost performance and ease of driving (compact car)

Ideal for those who commute daily or often travel with one or two people, and who prioritize fuel efficiency and maneuverability.

* Toyota Yaris / Honda Fit:
  * Features: A classic compact car that represents Japan. The Yaris is attractive for its overwhelmingly low fuel consumption (more than 30km/L in WLTC mode for hybrid vehicles) and brisk driving, while the Fit is characterized by its class-leading rear seat space and easy-to-use cargo space.
  * Advantages: For both, you can get a hybrid model or a grade equipped with extensive safety equipment (Toyota Safety Sense/Honda SENSING, etc.) for the low 2 million yen range.

## Points to consider when making a purchase

* Number of passengers and usage: You can't go wrong if you narrow it down based on how many people you usually ride (if you're carrying 4 or more people or carrying large luggage, choose Sienta or N-BOX, or if you're mainly carrying 1 or 2 people, choose Yaris or Yaris Cross).
