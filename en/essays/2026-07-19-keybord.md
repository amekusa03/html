# keyboard and mouse

## 2026.07.20 / Miscellaneous

I'm particular about keyboards.

## keyboard

I like keyboards that don't have 10 keys and have shallow strokes.
If the stroke is too deep, fatigue will build up in the back of the hand.
At home, I use Owltech's DWL-KB90PFlat.
My workplace is Elecom's Slint TK-TM10BPBK.

It is better to have close access to the mouse to type programs.
When I worked in accounting, I wanted a 10 key, but now I don't need one.

## mouse

For now, Buffalo's BSMBW325BK.
Although it is wireless, the battery does not run out easily and the time between slides is good.
To be honest, there are few opportunities to use the 5 button in Ubuntu.

## old person

As you probably know, before USB there was PS/2, and before that there was serial port.
The PS/2 port was round, so I had to line up the arrows and insert it to get it in.
There were many people who tried to force it in and ended up with bent pins.

## Modern keyboards and mice

It has evolved a little since USB, but
Surprisingly no difference.
Although smartphones have flip input and voice input,
The keyboard is still superior.
