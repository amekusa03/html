# PC virtual Google Home device

2026-08-02

## overview

If you can create a virtual Google Home device on your smartphone and connect it to your PC,
I thought it would be possible to control the power of my PC using just my smartphone and Google Home.

## background

Until now, I had been using ESP32 to control the power supply.
It all started when I suddenly noticed a smartphone next to me and thought, ``Huh, maybe I can use this?''

However, smartphones are not Matter devices, so
The biggest hurdle was getting Google Home to recognize it in the first place.

![alt text](/en/essays/2026-08-02-homedev.png)

## result

I was able to turn it on and off from my smartphone without any problems.
However, it is difficult to make it "appear" as a Google Home device.
Actually, to be honest, I didn't know how to do it.
