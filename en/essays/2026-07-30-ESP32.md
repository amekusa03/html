# Even ESP32 could not connect to ELM327

## 2026-07-30 / Development

## overview

There are things like this.
ESP32 (dual core 240MHz) + 1.9 inch IPS ST7789V3 (170 x 320px) for just under 2000 yen!
I had no choice but to buy this, so I put it into practice immediately.
However, behind this low price, there was an unexpected trap lurking.

## background

A good start, arriving the day after purchase.
Immediately rewrite the target and PIN definitions and attempt communication with great enthusiasm.

...However, the ACK never came back.
Suspecting the initialization of the flash, I fiddled with the dBm settings,
I tried every possible solution I could think of, but there was no response at all.

## Talk to AI

In times like these, perhaps the modern way is to ask AI first.
However, what comes back is
``Smartphone interference'' or ``Is your car's ACC turned off?''
Just bland generalizations.

Even though I am a gentle person, I ended up typing this.
“AI-san, don’t you trust me?”

Just when I was about to reach my limit, I suddenly picked up the ESP32 with the power on, and...
For just a moment, there was evidence of a connection in the log.
"Huh?" I thought, and when I brought my smartphone closer using the SCAN function I had installed,
Communication began with a bang.

The distance is only 10cm.

...You should be able to see it, please lend me your strength.
(I completely ignored the effective length of the antenna)
