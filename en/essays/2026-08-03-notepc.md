# I just want to listen to music on my laptop

## chance

Listening to music through the dynabook RX73's built-in speakers is, frankly, almost torture. It was impossible to ``enjoy music'' with something that only produced a crunchy sound, and I had been thinking about doing something about it for a while.

However, I don't have the energy or money to seriously build audio, so this time I decided to just do something with what I had on hand.

- PC：dynabook RX73
- Amplifier: DigiFi No. 7 appendix USB amplifier
- Speaker: Genuine Honda 17cm speaker

Normally I should have put it in an enclosure, but unfortunately I don't have any such parts on hand. This time, I gave up on that and started with a crude configuration, making only the speaker cables and plugging everything else in.

## Audition and baptism

First, I tried playing the song with the Windows volume set to standard.

It's a blast.

That's right, I had completely forgotten that this amp was meant to be operated at volume 1. I hurriedly turned the volume down to the lowest level and tried listening again.

As a result, it produces a very good sound considering it doesn't have an enclosure. To be honest, I wasn't expecting it so I was a little surprised. When I looked at the magnets on the speakers, they were quite small, and I was wondering if they would produce any sound pressure, but surprisingly, it turns out that's not the case.

However, as I was listening to the sample, I felt that it would be necessary to pair this amp with a speaker with a slightly higher impedance.

## In the end, 17cm was too big.

Once you're satisfied with everything, calmly review the structure.

No matter how you look at it, a 17cm speaker next to a laptop is disproportionately large. It's not easy to use and only hinders portability.

So when I was looking for another speaker, I came across the monaural Bluetooth speaker (WS001) that I had previously bought at Daiso. When I tried connecting it and listening to it, I found that if you just wanted to listen to some music, this would be enough.

This may be just the right amount for notebook PC usage. Above all, it's easy, and if you have an overnight business trip, you can bring this configuration with you.

## summary

- Successful departure from built-in speakers
- The 17cm+USB amplifier had good sound, but it was too exaggerated to be a companion for a notebook PC.
- In the end, I came to the dumb conclusion of Daiso's Bluetooth speaker.
- Not bad as a "divided configuration" for business trips
