# Security measures and advanced usage guide for older devices due to end of Android support

## 2026-07-21

## 1. Response to end of Android support and security enhancement measures

If you continue to use an OS that is no longer supported, the vulnerabilities of the OS itself will not be resolved, so it is essential to close the "attack entrance" and protect the communication route through settings.

### 1.1 Review basic security settings

* Enable Google Play Protect: Keep app scanning and threat detection always on and up to date from the Security screen.
* Tighten app permissions: Examine apps with sensitive permissions, such as location, camera, and microphone, and revoke any unnecessary permissions.
* Eliminate suspicious apps: Uninstall or disable apps you don't recognize or manufacturer-specific preinstalled apps (carrier apps, etc.) to stop them from working.

### 1.2 Security measures when connecting to public wifi

* Private DNS: Performs DNS encryption to prevent unauthorized access to DNS servers.
* VPN: By introducing a VPN, communications are encrypted and unauthorized access is prevented.
* Work profiles and multiple users: Separate apps and data to limit the scope of damage in the event of an emergency.

## 2. Ideas for reusing old terminals as “dedicated devices (sub devices)”

By switching to operation in a Wi-Fi environment, it can be revived as a device specialized for a specific purpose.

### 2.1 Daily/entertainment use

|Usage|Specific usage method|Supplementary|
|---|---|---|
|Music player|Utilize the offline storage function of Spotify and YouTube Music. |You can save the communication amount and battery of the main device. |
|Video playback device|Fixed to a stand to watch recipe videos in the kitchen or as a device for children. |It shows its true value in an environment where you don't mind getting dirty. |
|E-book reader|Machine dedicated to reading apps such as Kindle and Rakuten Kobo. |You can concentrate on reading by turning off notifications. |
|Digital photo frame|Use Google Photo's slideshow function and display with constant power supply. |Reuse as interior decoration. |

### 2.2 Business/Development/Creative Use

* Turn your PC into a sub-monitor: Display chat tools and system monitors as a wireless display.
* Left-hand device (macro pad): Dedicated shortcut buttons are placed to improve the efficiency of video editing and coding.
* High-resolution web camera: Connect a rear camera with higher performance than the laptop's built-in camera to the PC.
* Actual test terminal: Permanently installed to check operation during development and as an experimental environment using ADB.

### 2.3 Crime prevention/recording/safety purposes

* Network camera (monitoring): Transform into a pet camera. The motion detection function allows you to check from anywhere.
* Drive recorder (spare): Install a drive recorder app and install it in the car as a rear camera or backup.
* Disaster prevention smartphone: Install offline maps, radio apps, and emergency information apps, and keep it in your emergency bag along with your mobile battery.

## 3. Optimization procedures in operation

For successful reuse, it is important to reduce operational weight and manage physical deterioration (particularly batteries).

### 3.1 Terminal optimization work

1. Factory reset: Erases residual data and past settings to speed up operation.
2. Stop unnecessary apps: Turn off notifications and actions of apps running in the background to reduce the load on memory and battery. Actively disable manufacturer pre-installed apps.
3. Operation of a dedicated Google account: Create a sub-account separate from the main account to minimize the risk of personal information being leaked in the event of a security breach. Do not install apps that contain confidential information, such as banking, SNS, payment apps, etc.

### 3.2 Battery and thermal management

Concerns about keeping older terminals running all the time include battery deterioration and heat generation. Batteries in particular are prone to expansion, so the following measures are important.

* Charging management: To avoid maintaining the 100% state, power supply control using smart plugs prevents battery expansion.
* Heat runaway prevention: Especially when used as a drive recorder, the dashboard under direct sunlight tends to become hot, which can cause the app to stop or malfunction. Consider cooling by installing it at the air conditioner outlet.
* Physical check: Regularly check whether the back panel is lifted or deformed, and if any abnormality is found, immediately stop using it.

## conclusion

The end of support for an Android device does not mean the end of its lifespan. Security concerns can be resolved through ``purpose specialization'' and ``network separation.'' Old terminals are already ``small computers with high-performance sensors'' that we already have, and by redefining them as timers, monitors, cameras, or disaster prevention tools, they can continue to provide value.
