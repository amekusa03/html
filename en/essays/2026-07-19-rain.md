# To the rain man

## 2026.07.19

## conclusion

My name is Amakusa, but is it Naze Ame?
That's because he's a rain guy.

## background

Up until now, it has often rained when there are events.
There are times when I lose (?!) when there is a hare-man among the members.

## Noteworthy achievements

* It rains when I drive in a kart race. When it comes to group races, it only rains when it's my turn to run.
* When there was a lack of rain in Shikoku, I went on a trip and it rained. The proprietress of the inn was pleased and said, ``Thank you.''
* During the Nagano Olympics, I went skiing and it rained. The announcer said it was "rare."
* When I ride the Shinkansen for work, it has stopped several times due to heavy rain. My understanding is that Shinkansen trains often stop.
* I went to Kumejima for 5 days but couldn't go out for 4 days due to a typhoon. It turned around in the sky.
　Of course, we encountered typhoons after we returned home, and we encountered one typhoon three times.
