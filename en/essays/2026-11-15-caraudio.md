# Upgrading to a 2-Way 2.1-Channel System: A Step Out of the Car Audio Rabbit Hole

2026-11-15

## Escape from the Swamp?!

As planned in my previous post, I finally removed the rear speakers and repurposed those channels to add tweeters to the front. This marks the transition to a “2.1-channel” setup consisting of a 2-way front system plus a subwoofer.

## System Configuration

Following this work, my car's audio system now consists of the following components.

- **Head Unit**: Honda Genuine DOP Navigation System
- **DSP**：PUZU CZ-C7
- **Front Speakers**: Carrozzeria TS-F1740II
- **Tweeter**: Clarion SRH217
- **Tweeter Filter**: Nichicon Metallized Film Capacitor (2.2 μF, 100 V)
- **Subwoofer**: Carrozzeria TS-WX22A

## Thoughts: A sense of accomplishment comes before the sound

Once I had finished connecting everything, the first thought that came to mind was simply, “I’m overwhelmed.”
Mind you, that wasn’t in response to the sound that began to play, but rather to the fact that I had successfully completed the task of routing the tweeter wires.

As for the sound—which is the most important thing—it does sound good, but to be honest, the sense of accomplishment and exhaustion right after setting it up are still overwhelming, so I haven’t been able to evaluate it objectively yet.
I think I’ll stick with this setup for a while and see how it goes until my ears and mind have had a chance to settle down.
