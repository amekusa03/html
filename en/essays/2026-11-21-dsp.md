## The Main Cause of Muted Sound

So, why doesn't the sound come out cleanly?
One reason is that the same sound is "coming out from various places all over the place."

Take bass, for example. It comes from the subwoofer, but it’s actually also coming from the front door speakers, and in some cases, the rear door speakers are producing the same sound as well.
No matter how carefully you sound-dead the interior, if the same bass frequencies from various speakers mix together inside the car, it’s only natural that they’ll interfere with each other, resulting in a muddy, muffled sound.

What makes it even more troublesome is the “asymmetrical” environment of the driver’s seat.
In a right-hand-drive car, the right speaker is right next to you, while the left speaker is much farther away. Since there’s a physical time lag in how the sound reaches your left and right ears, no matter how finely you adjust the volume or equalizer, the sound will never be perfectly in sync.

## "DSP": The Savior and the Devil's Little Box

The **DSP (Digital Signal Processor)** is what resolves this physical contradiction in one fell swoop.
A DSP is a magical piece of equipment that uses digital processing to literally “directly manipulate and fine-tune” the sound from each individual speaker.

Specifically, it pulls off feats such as the following:

- **Crossover (Frequency Division)**
  This allows you to “leave the ultra-low frequencies to the subwoofer alone and prevent other speakers from producing bass they’re not capable of.” By assigning each speaker only the frequency range it excels at, you can dramatically eliminate muddiness in the sound.
- **Time Alignment (Sound Delay Adjustment)**
  This is the DSP’s greatest strength. It “deliberately delays” the sound from the speakers closest to the driver’s seat by a few milliseconds. As a result, the sound reaches the listener’s ears at “exactly the same moment” as the sound from the distant speakers and subwoofers, ensuring that the sounds from different speakers are perfectly synchronized.
- **Equalizer (Frequency Correction)**
  It can precisely target the frequency ranges where the doors and interior resonate, causing strange noises (rattling sounds), or smoothly adjust the sound to suit the in-car environment.
