# No sound from all car audio channels

2026-8-29

## background

While driving, all channels of the car audio suddenly stopped producing sound. There was a small popping noise at first, followed by silence.

The key point was that it had all channels, and my first suspicion was the head unit or DSP. First, try removing the DSP and connecting the head unit directly to the speakers. Still no sound.

After checking the power supply and speaker wiring one by one, I discovered an abnormality in the resistance value of the left rear speaker. When I tried connecting only the front speakers, the sound came out.

In other words, even if one speaker was shorted, the amplifier's protection was working on all channels. When I returned the rear speakers to the standard ones before replacement, the problem was easily resolved. Both the DSP and head unit were innocent. My guess turned out to be wrong, and I learned a lot this time as well.

## DSP reconfiguration

Additionally, the subwoofer's output was too high, so I also reviewed the DSP settings. I went back to basics and set everything except the filter to zero base with PASS settings.
