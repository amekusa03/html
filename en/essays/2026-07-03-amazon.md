# A story about canceling an item ordered on Amazon (Part 1)

2026.07.03 / Amazon

## background

At home, I use a utility knife and my fingers to cut the wiring.
But it's not pretty, and it's not smart.

## at work

The company has a wire stripper. Of course, I use it when I can.
Once you get used to this, you won't feel like cutting the coating with a utility knife anymore.

## solution

A simple version of the wire stripper was sold on Amazon for just under 300 yen.
I bought it immediately.

## failure

but. No matter how much I wait, it doesn't come.
At first I thought it couldn't be helped since it was cheap, but two months is a long time.
When I looked at the reviews, it seemed like everyone was in the same situation recently.

## cancel

When I consulted AMAZON's AI, it seemed like the only option was to cancel.
I canceled due to the flow of AI chat.

## look for a replacement

Well, what happened?
If you search online, Astro wire stripper costs 980 yen (shipping not included)
I want one, but I only use it at home once in a while, so I'm not sure about it.
Maybe I should go to the nearby Astro.
