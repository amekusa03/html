# A man who will deliver even if you cancel

## 2026-08-01

Previously, I talked about ordering parts for electronic work from Amazon. It didn't arrive for a while, so I canceled the order out of frustration, and then it arrived right after I canceled. And this time, the exact same thing happened again.

I felt like I was doing something really bad.

## This time's opponent

I ordered this time before canceling the previous item. I thought I had just bought it from a domestic vendor, but I noticed it for the first time when I was looking at the delivery status. "This is being shipped from overseas," he said.

## background

When I followed up on the delivery status, it appeared that it had arrived safely in Japan in about two weeks. Things are going well so far. However, after that, the estimated delivery date changed twice and three times, and in the end, only the sentence "Delivery date cannot be displayed" remained. This sentence inspired me to take the next action.

## second cancellation

In fact, the last time I canceled another product, it had already said "Delivery date cannot be displayed" and I had already inquired about this product. The answer I received was, "Please wait a few more days." Of course, I waited patiently. However, even after a few days, the message "Delivery date cannot be displayed" still appeared, so I finally inquired again and came to the conclusion that I had to make a decision.

![alt text](/en/essays/2026-08-01-amazon.png)

## after cancellation

After cancelling, I searched for the same item again with another vendor, and this time it arrived in just 3 days. The price was a little high, but considering the 6 week wait, it was cheap.

I had a lot of fun with it, and that's when I started thinking about putting it away.

In the mailbox was a package made overseas that looked familiar.

Yes, the item arrived 2 weeks after the cancellation and 8 weeks after the order was placed. Awesome cross-border delivery.

I made another inquiry and was successful, but I felt like it was summer.I remembered the day I turned in my summer vacation homework on September 2nd.
