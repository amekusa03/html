# Could not connect to EML327 with ESP32-C6

## 2026-07-23 / Development

## overview

Seriously, I overlooked it.
ESP32-C6 did not support Bluetooth Classic (SPP).
It only supported Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

## background

I was trying to communicate between ESP32-C6 and OBD2.
OBD2 supports Bluetooth Classic (SPP).
ESP32-C6 did not support Bluetooth Classic (SPP).
It only supported Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

## ending

To connect OBD2 with ESP32-C6, it was necessary to use an ESP32 series that supports Bluetooth Classic (SPP).
There is also ESP32-C3, but this is also not supported.

Yes, it's finished.

Among them, ESP-WROOM-32 compatible with Bluetooth Classic with display
I wanted to buy it, but I was out of budget right now.
