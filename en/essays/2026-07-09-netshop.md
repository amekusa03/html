# online shopping

2026.07.07 / Amazon

I had the opportunity to buy several small car accessories in bulk. Fuse power supply, bullet terminals, baffles, speakers, and control sound materials. Not all products are available at the same store, and if shipping costs and point redemptions are included, a simple price comparison may not be enough to make a correct decision. Therefore, we decided to calculate the ``evaluation value'' for each store, taking into account shipping costs and points, and compare them.

## The yardstick of evaluation value

Add the shipping cost to the amount and subtract the point redemption amount. That's all, but there were some items that made a difference between doing it and not doing it. In particular, since Regetrex had different sizes for each product, the evaluation value was calculated based on the same size per unit. If I ignored the size difference and just looked at the price, I would have made a wrong decision.
However, other biases remain: Yahoo chooses items that are nearly new or unused.
In addition, there may be cases where they are not completely the same, and this is included in the evaluation value.

## What I learned from graphing

When we graphed the evaluation values ​​for the five items, we could clearly see the ``habits'' of each store.

Rakuten had the lowest prices for four items: fuse power supplies, bullets, baffles, and Regetrex. Even if the price is high, once you subtract the points, it is consistently cheap for a wide range of items. Honestly, I was surprised to find that this was the most popular item among the ones I investigated this time.

Yahoo Auctions and Flea Market ranked second to or slightly behind Rakuten for most items, except for speakers, because they were second-hand items. Rating value 751. Compared to Amazon's 6217 and Rakuten's 5754, there is one digit difference. If it hits, it will hit on this scale. This is probably the characteristic of Yahoo's "bargain".

Amazon has a reassuring selection of products, but when it comes to price, it doesn't have the lowest prices for any item this time. When it came to speakers, it was in the highest category.

## Purchasing policy

The policy we derived from this was surprisingly simple.

- When it comes to products on Rakuten, you can think of it as the first choice.
- For products that are not available on Rakuten or for which there are few options, compare Yahoo and Amazon and choose the cheaper one.

The division of roles, such as ``Rakuten for standard items that can be found everywhere'', ``Yahoo for bargains'', and ``Keep Amazon as one of the comparison targets'' was clearly expressed in this small sample of 5 items.

## Conclusion

It was an interesting discovery that even though I was only shopping for five items, I could reveal the character of each store just by using one yardstick, the evaluation value. The next time I buy something in bulk, I'll use Rakuten as a base and compare Yahoo and Amazon as much as I need. This policy seems to work for the time being.
