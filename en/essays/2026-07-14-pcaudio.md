# PC modification Center SP replacement

2026.07.14

## assignment

PC Audio uses Yamaha's AV amplifier and surround set TSS-20,
The vocals are definitely weak.
Normally, the LR SP should be upgraded for music, but on Yahoo Auction
I got the NS-C110 at a cheap price, so I tried replacing the center SP first.

## thoughts

When I connected it right away and listened, there wasn't much of a difference.
The LR speakers are the main ones, and the center NS-C110 doesn't come out much.
When I turned on virtual surround, the sound came out from the center NS-C110, and I could hear the vocals clearly.

## solution

This is enough for me.
There is a temptation to upgrade the LR speakers, but placing them on your PC desk and listening at close range is not a very good environment.
For now, this is enough for me to listen to my favorite Jazz.

## Ongoing issues

* Equipment around PC
  The volume of the center SP and woofer is large, making it impossible to place other items.
  The PC is quite large when combined into a mini tower, and I can't put a printer on it, so I've made it wireless and put it on another shelf.
* PC audio output
  I am connecting with my PC's coaxial digital audio output, but when I update my PC, many models do not have digital audio output.
  In that case, is there no choice but to connect with a USB/optical digital conversion adapter?
