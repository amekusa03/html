# QD-OLED, Mini LED, Organic EL: Understanding TV display technology

2026-07-22

This memorandum summarizes the mechanisms, advantages, disadvantages, and suitability for each application of the three main display technologies: QD-OLED, Mini LED, and conventional organic EL (OLED).

* QD-OLED is a hybrid technology that combines the jet black of organic EL and the vivid color of quantum dots (QD), providing the highest level of visual experience available at the moment.
* Mini LED is a technology that significantly improves the ``black floating'', a weak point of LCDs, by minimizing the backlight of conventional liquid crystal displays (LCDs) and controlling it separately for each area (local dimming).
* Conventional organic EL (OLED) boasts perfect black, infinite contrast, and excellent response speed, but the challenge is balancing high brightness with burn-in risk.

The key to selection lies in the ``viewing environment'' and ``purpose.'' Mini LED is suitable for bright living rooms and long viewing periods, while QD-OLED and organic EL are ideal for watching movies and high-end gaming experiences that emphasize immersion.

## QD-OLED

QD-OLED is the next generation self-emissive display technology developed by Samsung Display.

### Mechanism and structure

Unlike conventional organic ELs (mostly white OLEDs with color filters), they use blue organic ELs as the light source.

* Wavelength conversion: Blue OLED light is transmitted through a quantum dot layer and converted into highly pure red (R) and green (G).
* RGB generation: Rather than blocking light with a filter, it converts the light particles themselves, resulting in extremely low light loss and high energy efficiency.

### Main benefits of QD-OLED

* Overwhelming color expression: Quantum dots produce particularly rich red and green colors, achieving a wider color gamut that is more vivid than conventional OLEDs.
* High brightness and contrast: While maintaining the "perfect black" of organic EL, the brightness (brightness), which was a weak point of conventional OLED, has been improved.
* High response speed: Boasting a response speed of 0.03ms class, there is almost no afterimage.
* Wide viewing angle: Provides a uniform image with little color change no matter what angle you view it from.

### Points to note (disadvantages) of QD-OLED

* Burn-in risk: There is still a risk of image retention due to long-term display of still images.
* High cost: Located in the premium price range, it is very expensive compared to LCDs of the same size.
* Anti-reflection measures: There are many glossy panels, so reflections from lighting may be a concern.

## Mini LED and local dimming: redefining the LCD

Mini LED is a method that pushes the limits of liquid crystal displays through advances in backlight technology.

### split control

The essence of Mini LED is "local dimming," which involves arranging thousands of tiny LEDs and controlling them in each area.

* Tightening of blacks: By physically turning off or dimming the backlight in dark areas of the image, the ``black floating'' characteristic of LCDs is suppressed.
* High brightness: Utilizing the powerful light emitting ability of LEDs, clear visibility can be maintained even in bright rooms with sunlight shining through.

### halo phenomenon

Unlike organic EL, which can control light emission on a pixel-by-pixel basis, Mini LED can only control on an area-by-area basis.

* Occurrence of bleeding: When there is strong light (subtitles, pointer, etc.) against a black background, the surrounding backlights are also turned on, which may cause a halo phenomenon (blooming) in which the light blurs.
* Dependence on algorithm: In addition to the large number of divisions (number of zones), the control algorithm of the video analysis processor has a large effect on the actual image quality.

## Recommended selections by purpose and lifestyle

### Movie/drama lovers: Organic EL/QD-OLED

Reason: The ``pitch black'' expression creates an immersive feeling similar to a movie theater. In particular, the gradation of dark scenes is overwhelming compared to backlit LCDs.
Recommended environment: A dimly lit room or dedicated theater room.

### Family living viewing: Mini LED

Reason: The screen is easy to see during the day, even in a bright living room with the curtains open. Furthermore, there is no need to worry about image retention caused by subtitles displayed on news programs.
Recommended environment: Living room facing south with plenty of sunlight, watching TV for long periods of time.

### Competitive gamer: QD-OLED / Organic EL

Reason: The response speed is overwhelmingly fast, so the outlines can be seen clearly even during intense movements such as FPS and fighting games.
Note: To take full advantage of 4K/120Hz or higher, an HDMI 2.1 compatible model and an ultra high-speed HDMI cable are required.

### Important checkpoints during implementation

In addition to panel technology, the following should be considered when selecting a product:

1. Quality of control engine: Even with the same Mini LED panel, the perceived image quality varies greatly depending on the manufacturer's unique video processing engine, such as SONY (natural control) or REGZA (excellent definition).
2. Brightness of the installation environment: Organic EL looks beautiful on the sales floor, but in a bright living room at home, the high brightness of Mini LED is often useful.
3. Confirming acoustic performance: Despite advances in video, flat-screen TV speakers have physical limitations. It is important to consider the presence or absence of unique technology such as an "acoustic surface" that vibrates the screen itself, as well as the addition of a sound bar.
4. Securing a delivery route: Large models of 65 inches or more are packed so large that it is necessary to measure in advance whether they will be able to pass through elevators or entrances.
5. Consider long-term warranties: OLED panels tend to be expensive to repair, so it is highly recommended to purchase an extended warranty of 5 years or more.

## conclusion

There is no "one correct answer" when choosing a display. QD-OLED is suitable for enthusiasts who seek the best image quality, Mini LED is suitable for households that value versatility and practicality, and conventional organic EL is suitable for those who value design and cost balance. It is important to accurately understand your own viewing style.
