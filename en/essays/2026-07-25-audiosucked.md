## When you hit the nail on the head

When looking at the NS-C110, there is a tweeter in the middle and two woofers on the left and right.
If this is stereo. . . I sigh. . .
This is probably 10cm. Huh? Speaking of which. . .
I feel like the Pioneer TS-1019 sitting in the closet was also a 10cm full range.
Could it have been done by replacing it? !
(TS-1019 is for cars)

## I did it

30 years ago since the closet? ! I discovered the TS-1019 that I bought.
The cone hasn't broken, and it's durable enough for a car.
I immediately replaced the woofer with the NS-C110.
Distributes the output of TSS-20 directly to the front L/R 2ch.
Immediately, when you listen to music, the resolution increases! There's a bit of a lack of bass, but it's very easy to listen to.

## result

Anyway, I told my family and they were impressed.
It's refreshing and easy to listen to.
You did it! Great success!

## And the current configuration

- Amplifier: Yamaha TSS-20
- Front LR speaker: TS-1019 Enclosure NS-C110
- Center, subwoofer, satellite speakers: TSS-20
- Motherboard:GIGABYTE GA-H87-HD3
   - Connect the Realtek ALC892's S/PDIF output to the TSS-1's coaxial digital input. Handmade digital cables.

## TS-1019 specifications

- Speaker configuration: 10cm injection molded PP cone woofer, 4.2cm cone tweeter
- Maximum instantaneous input: 50W
- Rated input: 10W
- Playback frequency band: 50-24,000Hz
- Output sound pressure level: 90dB/W (1m)
- Impedance: 4Ω
- Weight: 0.5kg (1 piece)
