# [Canceled] Smart PC Project

2026-05-08 / C# / Matter over Wi-Fi 

Integrating PC power management systems with Matter over Wi-Fi

## About this Project

This project is an attempt to integrate ESP32-C6 into Ubuntu PC and make it compatible with the latest smart home standard "**Matter**".
Our goal is to make it possible to seamlessly manage and operate the power status of PCs just like other home appliances.

This project was canceled due to lack of skills.

Reason
・I purchased an ESP32 with an LCD, but after a lot of testing because I had mistaken the empty port, I suspected the resistance of the photocoupler, and after soldering it many times,
I destroyed the photo coupler. (Pattern peeling off)
・I created a function that was intended to be a safety function so that it would turn off if the status became inconsistent.
I repeatedly hit the switch and started and shut down my PC.
・I didn't know the specifications of the power LED and power SW in the M/B of the PC, but I tried to measure them and match them, but failed. (I didn't understand clearly)
