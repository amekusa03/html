# Checking the operation of the car subwoofer

2026.07.10

## successful bid

I made a successful bid on a subwoofer called Carrozzeria TS-WX22A on Yahoo! Auctions.
Operation not confirmed, scratches, very cheap.
I want to check the operation immediately after it arrives, but there is no cable.
Looking at the power connector, I guess it's a MOLEX 6P,
I checked it with the PCI Express of the PC power supply I had on hand, and bingo!
Of course, the wiring is different, so I investigated and found the following.
I checked with a circuit tester that there was no short circuit, and connected it to a 14V DC power supply.
Audio is connected to Line In from the earphone terminal of your smartphone.

![Connector processing](essays/2026-07-10-connecter.png)

## panic

There is no sound.
My imagination is that there should be a small pop noise the moment you turn it on.
Even if I set the volume on my smartphone to MAX, it doesn't come out.
Actually, the Remote terminal was not common to 1 and 4, but only number 4.
Since GND and BAT were common vertically, I thought Remote was also common and connected it to number 1.
I connected +14V to No. 4 and the problem disappeared.
(The diagram has been corrected.)

## be on one's mind

You can rest assured that it is not a junk item and can proceed with the installation.
There are two points of concern.

- There is no pop noise when the remote is turned on, but there is a pop noise when it is turned off.

・ 12V (MAX14V?) and 10A is a bit scary wiring. Should I use the wire from Daiso's household 100V tap? (If I'm going to buy a good wire, I'll also buy a new unit.)
