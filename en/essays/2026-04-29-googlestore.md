# Android app distribution policy

2026/04/29

Currently, the app I developed is not distributed on Google Play Store. One reason is that continuous quality assurance is difficult for individual developers.
In addition, the Google Play Store has become stricter in its review.

## Distribution on Google Play Store

With recent requirements, maintaining the evaluation period and the number of people evaluating is a very high hurdle for individuals.
Additionally, the Google Play Store is scheduled to begin reviewing app updates starting in September 2026, and distribution is expected to become even more difficult in the future.
Therefore, I decided against publishing it on the Google Play Store and considered distributing it on F-Droid instead.

## Distribution on F-Droid

F-Droid only offers open source apps, unlike Google Play Store.
Also, unlike the Google Play Store, F-Droid is not subject to strict screening.
This allows users to use apps with privacy and security in mind.
However, as we continued to verify the latest conditions, we discovered that even F-Droid could not be installed.
Therefore, we have now given up on distributing it on F-Droid and are only releasing the source code on GitHub.
