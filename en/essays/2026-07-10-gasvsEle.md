## Introduction

Energy supply and hot water equipment for general households has diversified from the traditional two main supply methods of gas and electricity. As electricity liberalization progresses, it is important to understand the characteristics, advantages, and disadvantages of each option before making the optimal choice. Here, we looked into what you should choose if you were to build a new house (although you have no plans at all).

## Economical

When comparing the total costs over a 10-year period, gas water heaters still tend to be the cheapest in city gas areas, but in propane gas (LP gas) areas and homes with solar power generation installed, it has been found that installing EcoCute (electric type) is advantageous.

## Subsidy system

Under the ``Water Supply Energy Saving 2026 Project'', subsidies of tens of thousands of yen will be provided for the introduction of high-efficiency water heaters. Energy optimization is being promoted, with the installation of an "Internet connection function" becoming a mandatory requirement from FY2026.

## At the time of disaster

In the event of a disaster, electricity is the lifeline that can be restored the quickest (about a week). On the other hand, LP gas has the advantage of being a "distributed energy" and can be quickly restored through individual inspections and can be used continuously by stockpiling. The combination of EV (electric vehicle) and V2H (vehicle to home) is effective as an emergency power source during power outages.

## safety

Gas stoves make it easy to adjust the heat level based on visibility, but the risk of fire is high. Although IH cooking heaters have a low risk of fire, it is difficult to adjust the heat level based on visibility. The latest models come standard with safety features such as Si sensors and forget-to-turn-off prevention functions.

## Economic analysis

Electricity rates across the country are increasing by about 4-5% compared to the previous year, and energy selection has become an issue that is directly linked to household budgets.

A comparison of the initial cost and running cost, assuming the lifespan of the water heater (approximately 10 years), is as follows.

| Item | EcoCute | City gas (Eco Joes) | LP gas (Eco Joes) |
| - | - | - | - |
| Initial cost (estimate) | 400,000 to 600,000 yen | Approximately 100,000 yen | Approximately 100,000 yen |
| Annual utility costs | Approximately 69,000 yen | Approximately 73,000 yen | Approximately 131,000 yen |
| Total cost for 10 years | Approximately 1.15 to 1.2 million yen | Approximately 880,000 yen (lowest price) | Approximately 1.42 million yen |

* City gas area: If there is no solar power generation, gas water heaters are the most economical option due to the low initial cost.
* LP gas area: Hot water supply costs are extremely high, so replacing it with EcoCute can save approximately 60,000 yen per year, and the initial cost can be recovered in a few years.
* Houses with solar power generation installed: Since the unit price of electricity purchased (approximately 28 yen/kWh or more) is higher than the unit price of electricity sold (approximately 16 yen/kWh), the most economical option is to use the "daytime heating mode" and consume the surplus electricity by yourself.

Businesses are also shifting to electrification due to the risk of rising gas prices, and especially for medium-sized businesses with high demand for hot water supply, energy costs can be significantly reduced by installing commercial heat pumps. Even in simple calculations without subsidies, there are scenarios in which small-scale facilities can expect to recover their investment in about three years.

## public support system

The government will continue to provide generous support for the introduction of high-efficiency water heaters in fiscal 2026.
The following basic subsidy amounts are set depending on the type and performance of the equipment to be introduced.

* EcoCute: 70,000 yen/unit (up to 100,000 yen if additional requirements are met)
* Hybrid water heater: 100,000 yen/unit (up to 120,000 yen if additional requirements are met)
* Ene Farm: 170,000 yen/unit

New requirements for 2026: Aiming to standardize the effective use of renewable energy by linking with weather data, etc., it is now mandatory to have an "Internet connection function."

Additional subsidies that can be used in conjunction with the removal allowance include removal of electric storage heaters (+40,000 yen) and electric water heaters (+20,000 yen).
Additionally, if your home is certified under the Future Eco Housing 2026 Project, you may receive a subsidy of up to 1,100,000 to 1,250,000 yen (installation of solar power generation and HEMS is required).
Systems that can be used in conjunction with government subsidies are being implemented in some regions, such as the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's ``Tokyo Zero Emi Points'' (12,000 points).

## Lifeline recovery speed

* Electricity: Almost all areas were restored in about a week. Repairs to power lines and utility poles can be performed more quickly than repairs to underground pipes, so recovery is the quickest.
* City gas: Recovery may take several weeks to several months as it is necessary to confirm the damage to underground pipes.

Since LP gas containers (cylinders) are installed in each individual household, there are the following advantages.

* After a disaster occurs, restoration can be completed faster than city gas or electricity through individual investigations and inspections.
* If two 50 kg containers are installed, the first container can be used for approximately one month or more (effect of under-eaves inventory).
* It can be quickly supplied to evacuation centers and temporary housing, and contributes to hot water supply, cooking, and power generation.

V2H systems that utilize electric vehicles (EVs) as "running storage batteries" are extremely effective as a countermeasure against power outages.

* Supply capacity: Nissan Leaf (40kWh/62kWh) can provide about 2 to 4 days worth of electricity for a typical household.
* Advantages: You won't be affected by long lines at gas stations or sales restrictions, and you'll be able to secure power for your home as soon as power is restored.

## Comparison of safety and convenience in equipment selection

When selecting cooking equipment and water heaters, it is essential to consider safety and lifestyle in addition to cost.

### Safety risks of gas stoves vs. IH cooking heaters

| Risk factors | Gas stove | IH cooking heater |
| - | - | - |
| Fire risk | Be careful of clothing igniting. | The risk of fire is low because there is no flame. |
| Oil ignition | Si sensor detects temperature. | There is a risk that the sensor will not be able to catch up due to rapid heating. |
| Burns | The area around the trivet and flame becomes hot. | After cooking, the plate becomes hot due to residual heat (not obvious from appearance). |
| Dry heating | Automatic extinguishing is delayed in high temperature stir-fry mode. | There is a risk that the sensor may malfunction due to deformation of the bottom of the pot. |

### Choices that suit your lifestyle

Reasons for choosing to use it in combination with gas: It is chosen when the overwhelming drying speed and quality of the gas clothes dryer ``Kenta-kun'' is important, or when using it in conjunction with an alternative method such as a cassette stove in the event of a power outage to diversify risk.
Reasons for choosing all-electricity: Suitable when centralizing utility costs, ease of cleaning IH, compatibility with solar power generation, and utilization of nighttime discount plans (however, as of 2026, care must be taken when reviewing plans).

## conclusion

The choice of hot water supply and energy equipment goes beyond a simple discussion of ``which is cheaper''; it is a judgment on how to prepare for future energy price hikes and how to build peace of mind in the event of an earthquake.

The service life of a water heater is approximately 10 years, and if you rush to replace it after it breaks down, you won't be able to make a thorough comparison. If 10 years have passed, it would be a good idea to consider early consideration, including the use of subsidies.

The optimal solution for each situation is as follows.

* No city gas + solar power: Gas water heater is economical.
* Using LP gas: Great savings by replacing with EcoCute.
* With solar power generation: Eco-cute + daytime heating is the strongest combination.

Regardless of which heat source you choose, it's a good idea to know in advance how to use the water from your cassette stove or the EcoCute's hot water tank (domestic water) in the event of a power outage or water outage.
