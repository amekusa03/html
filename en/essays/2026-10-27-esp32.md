# At a Complete Loss: The Night the Brand-New ESP32 Went Silent

2026-10-27

## background

Today, I once again wrote some code as ideas came to me, and tried to flash my brand-new program onto a brand-new ESP32.
...But my PC wouldn't respond at all. It simply wouldn't recognize the device.

## A tough battle

I suspected a faulty cable, so I tried switching to a different communication cable, but that didn't work either.
It didn't respond even when I tried a different USB port.
It didn't react at all—not even when I tried booting while holding down the BOOT button, or when I tried forcing it into boot mode by connecting the ESP32's familiar GPIO9 pin directly to GND.

This is exactly what it means to be completely helpless.

## Withdraw for now

Looks like that's it for tonight. If I push any further, I'll just get bogged down.

Let's put the decision off until tomorrow.
