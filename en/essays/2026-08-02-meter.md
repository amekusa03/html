2026.08.02

## Until before

I wanted to try acquiring car data using OBDII, so I started ordering parts (ELM327) and creating an Android app for the past two months.
It turns out that Bluetooth Classic is not supported in ESP32-C6.
I had to purchase ESP32-WROOM, and even then I was only able to communicate at 20cm.

## current situation

Anyway, I started collecting data using an Android app today.

## Assumption

I had made many assumptions.
Suction temperature, pressure, etc. may not be compatible depending on the manufacturer or car model.
As expected, I couldn't get those numbers.

## result

The only information I was able to obtain was speed, rotational speed, water temperature, and accelerator opening.
Although it was less than I expected, if I had all this...

## analysis

My car is a hybrid. In other words, there are times when the engine is not rotating.
This is expected.
However, the number of rotations. It remains constant even when the speed increases.
That's right, hybrids only generate electricity at low speeds, so the engine seems to generate electricity at constant rotation.
I see.
The accelerator opening is also linked to the rotation speed.
I see. Wait, what? Is that so?

In other words, the rotation speed and accelerator opening are completely controlled.
A condition that has nothing to do with running.

Isn't this only usable for water temperature?
Well, the water temperature only goes up as the engine rotates.
Seriously?

Just look at the speedometer and let the computer do the rest.
