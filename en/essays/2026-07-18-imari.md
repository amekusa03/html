# Imari dyeing

2026.07.18

## Poor name

Antiques tend to be expensive.
I like Imari's dyeing.
When many people hear the term "Imari Sometsuke," they imagine something extremely expensive.
The items I collect cost less than 1,000 yen each and display on my everyday table.
The period is from the late Edo period to the early Meiji period.
If anything, it might be better to call it an old tool.

## chance

One day, I ate at a somewhat upscale Japanese restaurant and had a wonderful meal.
In addition to the food, the dishes were dignified and complemented the meal.

Around that time, I had plates lined up at home that I had bought at 100 yen shops.
When I went to a fancy shop in my neighborhood, they were selling them for several thousand yen each, but I just couldn't afford them.

At that time, I stumbled into an antique shop and found a small Imari dyed plate that fit me perfectly.
When I asked the price, it was 500 yen each, so I bought it right away and took it home.

## recent years

Nowadays, I often look for them at flea markets.
I got one for 50 yen today.

Once I started buying several pieces, I started coming across the same ones more and more.
Actually, I only want one or two pieces, but I can't bear to break up the set.
I end up buying as much as I can.

Ko-Imari's Sometsuke may have a great name, but to me he is just a close friend.
