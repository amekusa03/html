# I tried using Dual SIM.

2025-12-30

## overview

Rakuten Mobile suits me.
Lifestyle has a big influence, but the only time I'm out of service is when I go deep in the mountains where I can't see the town, and I can basically connect, and since phone calls are free and I commute by car, I rarely use more than 3GB even if I use packet data.

## background

However, this rarely happens several times a year.
Basically, I only climb low mountains, but if you climb a mountain that is about an hour's drive away, you will be out of range.
Docomo and au are places where you can barely have one or two antennas, but if you stay in such a place for a long time, you may be worried.
I also sometimes go on day trips to the mountains by myself, which makes me a little nervous.
On the other hand, if I go on a trip and the inn doesn't have Wi-Fi, my packet usage will be slightly over 3GB near the end of the month.

There are two reasons for this.
However, I was actually very interested in Dual SIM.
Nowadays, if you buy packets every six months, you can secure a phone number.
When I asked the AI, it praised it as a good method.

Once you have all the reasons, apply now.
Download the povo app and quickly complete the procedure.
There are now two antenna marks on your smartphone.

## Consideration

Judgment is required here.
Should I use Rakuten or Povo to use packets outside?
Rakuten is fast, but I would like to use less than 3GB per month.
povo is 128kbps unless you buy packet.
First, I tried using povo for packet communication and going out.
My car has navigation and I listen to music via USB, but I really want to use Android Auto.
I have a Google Nest Hub and SwitchBot at home, and can control home appliances from Android Auto.
You can also use calendars and LINE.
It's not really used much, but it's interesting.

## result

I was able to listen to music on YouTube Music with Android Auto enabled.
There is no response when I give a voice command to set a destination...
When I requested the song I wanted to listen to via audio, there was no response, and after a while it responded that it was unable to connect.
After all, it seemed impossible to view a map, listen to music, and execute voice commands at 128kbps.
I decided to use Rakuten Mobile when I go out.
However, if you think it will exceed 3GB/month, you can switch.
I added a macro to MacroDroid so that switching could be done with one push.

I still can't get used to the fact that my smartphone has two antennas. 📶📶
