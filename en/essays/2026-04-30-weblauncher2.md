# Android Web Launcher 2

2026/04/30 kotlin

Awacha is an Android app that allows you to launch and manage specific websites as if they were independent apps. As a successor to Rancha (5 slots, 1 layer), group management based on a hierarchical structure has been introduced.

## About this Project

The previous "5 limit" has been significantly expanded, and a folder structure with up to 4 layers (16 slots of 4 x 4 per screen) has been introduced. Now you can organize and manage a large number of shortcuts.
This allows you to easily access certain web pages. The name ``Awacha'' comes from the fact that this launcher was born in Shizuoka, which is famous for its tea production.

## Key Features

- **Hierarchical management: **Uses a folder format that can be nested up to 4 levels. 
- **Hot water cup design: **Color-coded cylindrical icons are used for each slot. Customizable through an intuitive color panel. 
- **Automatic Favicon acquisition: **Automatically acquires the site icon from the URL and places it in the center of the teacup. 
- **Dedicated mode: **After launching the URL, the launcher itself will finish automatically and will not remain in the background. 
- **Breadcrumbs: **Even when the hierarchy is deep, icon display and text navigation make it easy to understand your current location and jump to higher levels. 
- **Dynamic shortcuts: **Shortcuts created on the Android home screen will be automatically synced when edited within the app.

## Known Issues / Out of Scope

- The behavior of updating and displaying home screen shortcuts depends on the specifications of the launcher app (home app) you are using. Dynamic updates are reflected immediately with NovaLauncher etc., but some pre-installed launchers may have restrictions.
