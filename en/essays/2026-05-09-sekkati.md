# SekkaTi

2026/05/09  / Avalonia UI / .NET

A memo pad for impatient people. Just take it out, write on it, and put it away. 

- ** Instant launch with hotkey: ** Call anytime from OS shortcut settings. 
- **Autosave: **Automatically saves content and exits when focus is lost. 
- **No need to manage files: **Automatic naming with timestamps, so you don't have to worry about thinking about names. 
- **Time series reference: **Quickly view past notes in units such as "today" and "yesterday". 
- **Resident type: **Waits in the background and prevents double activation.
