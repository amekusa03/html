# Laws of saturation difference and environmental control that affect plant growth

2026.07.13 / Nature Notes

When evaluating temperature and humidity data obtained on farms, we have compiled important points based on reliable sources regarding the relationship between plant growth and temperature and humidity.

## Interrelationship between temperature, humidity, and saturation difference

In order to maximize plant growth, especially photosynthesis, it is extremely important to manage not only temperature and humidity, but also the balance between the two, the ``saturation differential (VPD)''.

- **Tomato management goals**: Aichi prefecture guidelines recommend maintaining daytime humidity at 75-80% and saturation difference at 5-8 g/m³
- **Opening and closing of stomata**: When the saturation difference becomes large and it gets too dry, plants close their stomata to prevent water stress.
- This reduces CO₂ absorption and limits photosynthesis.

## Correlation with growth and growth indicators

The influence of environmental factors on the physiological state of plants is shown by the following numerical correlations.

- **Relationship with biopotential**: Studies using Schefflera have found a strong negative correlation between plant biopotential and temperature (r = -0.69), and a positive correlation with humidity (r = 0.469). In other words, the potential tends to decrease as the temperature increases, and the potential tends to increase as the humidity increases.
- **Stalk diameter variation (SDV)**: Plant stem diameter variation (SDV) is highly sensitive to surrounding environmental changes and moisture conditions and is utilized for plant-based irrigation schedule management.

## Optimization of growth through environmental control

Appropriate temperature and humidity control directly affects yield and quality.

- **Effect of humidification**: When the weather is dry in early spring, using mist spray to reduce the saturation difference will reduce the wilting of the stems and leaves. Tomato fruit cracking (a phenomenon in which the skin cracks) due to changes in dryness and humidity will also be reduced.
- **Promoting photosynthesis**: If the airflow velocity and humidity are appropriate, the stronger the light, the more effective the photosynthesis promotion effect of CO₂ application will be.

Plant growth is determined by plants sensing and responding to the ``air dryness'' (saturation difference), which is a combination of temperature and humidity, through the opening and closing of their stomata and changes in bioelectrical potential.
