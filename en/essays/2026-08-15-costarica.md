# Unauthorized access?

2026-07-20

## There was a disgusting log

When I looked at the server's access log, I noticed a request that shouldn't have been there. Moreover, multiple queries are running from one location. When I looked into the source, it turned out to be Costa Rica in Central America.

There is no evidence that it has been cracked, and no important resources are stored in it in the first place, so there is no actual damage. Still, there's something unsettling about not knowing my purpose.

## security reality

We operate within the free tier as much as possible. As for security, the only thing we have in place is something that can be tracked later - checking logs.

This time too, they were unable to follow Costa Rica. At least it was a relief that Google detected some of it.

If this is a site that handles personal information or a corporate site, the story is different. This made me realize once again that even more stringent measures are needed.
