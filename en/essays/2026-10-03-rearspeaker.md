# Honda Genuine Aftermarket Rear Speakers

2026-10-03

## background

A while back, my rear speakers (Gathers GS-5060DL) suddenly stopped working. I tried switching back to the stock speakers, but the more I looked into it online, the more I realized there’s no end to the list of great options. I was at a loss as to what to do.

Just then, I came across a set of front speakers for the N-WGN on Yahoo! Auctions. From the front, they looked exactly like the stock Fit speakers, but the magnet size was completely different. “This is definitely going to work,” I thought. Almost as soon as I was convinced, I clicked to win the auction. Even with shipping included, it was just under 1,500 yen. At that price, I wouldn’t regret it even if it didn’t work out.

## Installation

![Speaker Comparison](2026-10-03-rearspeaker1.JPG)

![Speaker Comparison](2026-10-03-rearspeaker2.JPG)

I installed them right away and gave them a listen. First, I routed the audio signal to the rear speakers.

Huh? The sound is clearly different from before.

The bass has improved, and the muffled sound is gone. It was a resounding success. While they’re nowhere near as good as Carrozzeria speakers, they’re more than enough for listening in the back seat. Actually, I wonder if those speakers were the ones causing the noise all along. It feels like the sound quality has improved across the entire speaker system.

## conclusion

I recommend this to Fit owners. It lets you improve the sound quality in the rear with virtually no risk.
