# Lunch Memo

2026/07/01 / kotlin

I often forget my lunch schedule, so I created an Android app to manage it.

## About this Project

Lunch Memo is an Android app that manages your daily lunch schedule and reservations.
We have devised a way to quickly leave notes using calendar-style card operations.
A noon notification will notify you of today's schedule.

## Key Features

- **Smart Tracking:** Swipe through calendar-style cards to quickly jot down your lunch.
- **Lunch notification:** Every day at 12:00, you will be notified of that day's lunch memo.
- **Modern design:** Full use of Material 3, smooth card animations and gradient backgrounds.
- **Supports notification permissions:** Supports notification permissions for Android 13 and above.
- **Clean data retention:** Automatically organize past notes to keep the app nimble.

## Setup

1. Open the project in Android Studio (recommended for Ladybug or newer).
2. Run Gradle Sync.
3. Run your app on an Android device or emulator.
