# don't blame the engineer

2025-12-13

## Security holes and software

I heard on the news that ransomware is rampant. First of all, I would like to express my deepest sympathies to all the web and infrastructure engineers out there.

Actually, I have a friend who works in the same industry. I was curious and sent him a LINE message.

Me: “Were you safe from the security holes in React and Node.js?”

Friend: “I applied the security patch.”

What, were you okay? I feel safe for now.

However, there are probably 50,000 such engineers in the world, so it must have been very difficult for them. It's one thing if it's a bug you caused, but if you're being blamed for a hole in someone else's code, and then it's hacked, you might not just get scolded. I don't think it's a fuss that would be anything but a mess.

## System reliability

People all over the world take it for granted that software works. If they don't move or if a hole is poked into them, they are immediately treated as criminals. In the past, I used to call SEs "Suimasen Engineers." It seems like a joke, and I think it's hitting the nail on the head. It's really Nanbo's job to apologize.

When it comes to microcomputer engineers, the situation is even more severe. Once a bug is leaked, it's so important that you might even consider changing jobs. It's understandable, since in the world of hardware, it's not something that can be fixed at a later date.

Microsoft's increased use of Windows Update may be providing some relief to PC engineers. This is because even if something happens, there is an escape route that can be fixed with an update later. However, there is still a tough task ahead.

## User perspective

If possible, I would like people to refrain from blaming the engineers too much or asking them too many questions about this incident. It's like taxi drivers being blamed for a car recall. It's the same as if you approached the person who was just driving the car and asked, ``Why did the car break?'' they wouldn't be able to answer.

We will take this matter seriously, so please cooperate with us.

After all, crackers are the worst.
