# How I Got Sucked into the World of Audio — In Pursuit of the “Original Sound” on Ubuntu [Speaker Cable Edition]

2026-10-20

## Before I knew it,

I thought I was satisfied after changing the speakers.
But the bass sounded a little muddy, and I felt like the mid and high frequencies weren't coming through clearly.

While browsing the internet, I came across some opinions suggesting that the speaker cable might be the problem. It’s true—the cable I’m using now is a thin one I repurposed from another audio setup.

After doing some research, it seems that cables actually have a surprisingly significant impact on sound quality.
On the other hand, many people say that “OFC cables are good enough.” But it was precisely this divide in opinion that led me to get deeply hooked on the subject.

In the end, I decided to buy a Belden 8460 speaker cable.
Of course, to keep it as short as possible, I cut it to 1 meter.

All that's left is to install the connectors I ordered separately.
It looks like I've really gotten sucked into the audio rabbit hole.
