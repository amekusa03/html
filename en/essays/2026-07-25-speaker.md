## Points to note

Released in 2001, this is an ultra-compact 2-way bookshelf speaker designed for use in magnetically shielded theaters (originally a sister model to the center model called ``NS-C10MM''), aiming to reproduce powerful and crisp theater sound.
The crossover is quite high at 7kHz, and the 9cm woofer is designed to handle a wide range of mid-to-high frequencies. The low frequency range is slightly higher at 75Hz, and the volume needs to be adjusted.
It is currently out of production, so you can get it used. It is necessary to check for aging deterioration such as urethane edges.

If space is your top priority, both the NS-10MMT and NS-B210 are strong ultra-compact candidates. The difference is that the NS-10MMT has a sharpness suitable for theater use, and the NS-B210 has a simple and straightforward closed-back type, so it seems like it will be a matter of preference. If you want to listen to music, use the NS-B210.

Being a closed type is advantageous for music applications. The NS-10MMT has a fairly high crossover of 7kHz, and has a bass reflex design with a 9cm woofer that handles a wide range of mid-to-high frequencies, so it was more suited for theater use, with an emphasis on localization and sharpness of sound effects. The NS-B210 is a sealed type with 8cm full range x 2, so there is less phase disturbance, making it easier to reproduce the texture of vocals and musical instruments.
The size is also about the same class as the NS-10MMT (105 x 215 x 132 mm), so it still satisfies the space-saving requirement.
It can also be mounted on a wall or fixed with a bracket, so if the depth of your desk is tight, you have the option of installing it on the wall.

By switching from the NS-C110 to the NS-B210, the design will change from a design that emphasizes the vocal range for center use to a more straightforward stereo playback design, so it should be easier to listen to music in general (instrumental and spacious songs other than vocals).
Although the volume of the low frequency range needs to be adjusted according to the caliber of 8cm full range, when used in combination with the TSS-20's YST subwoofer, the configuration is able to compensate for this deficiency.
