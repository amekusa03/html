# Packaging LunchMemo and Upgrading to Version 2.0

2026-11-14

## An app that has become a natural part of my daily life without me even noticing

Of all the apps I’ve developed so far, the one I use most frequently every day is “LunchMemo.”
It’s become such an integral part of my life that, on the rare occasion I forget to make an entry in LunchMemo, I rely on it so heavily that I’m genuinely confused the next day because I can’t remember my schedule.

## Installation via APK Package

On a whim, I decided to try creating an APK file for version 2.0.
I remember having trouble installing it before due to restrictions on third-party apps, but when I tried again, although a security warning popped up, the installation went through surprisingly smoothly—almost too easily.

This way, you can easily set it up without having to go through the trouble of connecting to a development environment. Since I went to the trouble, I went ahead and published the package on the GitHub release page.
