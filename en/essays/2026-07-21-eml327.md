# OBD2 ACC power supply

## 2026-07-21 / Car

## overview

The OBD2 adapter ELM327 is always powered,
If you connect it and turn on the power immediately, the battery may be drained.
This is not a problem, so I decided to switch to ACC power.

## background

If you search on the internet, you will find people with similar problems.
As a solution, I removed the constant power supply from the ELM327's power supply pin 16.
Connect the audio ACC power instead.
This should be able to connect to the car's power supply.

## process

* First, remove the cover, which is surprisingly difficult. Insert a pin etc. into the gap.
　Also, if it comes off, cut the nail so that it will be easier to remove in the future.
![alt text](essays/2026-07-21-eml327-1.png)
* Remove PIN 16. Use needle-nose pliers to bend it and remove it.
* Drill a hole in the case. Connect the new ACC power here.
* Wire a new power line to PIN 16.
![alt text](essays/2026-07-21-eml327-2.png)
* Do not connect the ACC power supply, but plug it into the OBD2 adapter and confirm that the power does not turn on.
* Connect the ACC power supply and check that the power turns on.

## solution

When I checked the operation, the LED turns off when the car is turned off.
The LED lights up when the engine is turned on.
This seems to prevent the battery from dying.

## awareness

The LED of this ELM327 flashes blue during communication.
It's kind of embarrassing when I drive down country roads at night.
I think I'll either remove the LED next time or put some black tape on it.
