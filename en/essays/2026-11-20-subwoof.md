# The Car Audio Rabbit Hole, Part 5: ~Finally Adding a Subwoofer~

2026-11-20

## I should have been satisfied, but for some reason, I still feel like something's missing.

Once I’d completed the sound-deadening work from last time, the rattling in the doors subsided, and the car finally began to exude the aura of a true “car audio” system.
There was a time when I thought, “Now that I’ve gotten this far, I’m completely satisfied—my quest for the perfect car audio system is finally complete.”

However, once you’ve gotten used to listening to it for a while, there comes a moment when you suddenly start to feel something’s off.
“...Huh? Doesn’t the bass sound a little lacking?”

Of course, the aftermarket speakers I installed have a wide frequency response that rivals that of home audio systems. However, this isn’t a quiet listening room—it’s the interior of a moving car.
Car-specific noises—such as road noise, engine noise, and the deep bass from the muffler—mercilessly drown out the bass coming from the speakers (this is what’s known in audio terminology as the “masking effect”).

"No, I really shouldn't take on any more..." I tell myself, wrestling with the decision in my head, but in the end, I find myself staring at online shopping sites night after night, and before I know it, I've clicked "Buy."
That's right—it's the arrival of the "subwoofer," the miracle cure that solves the lack of bass in one fell swoop.

## More than sound quality, the most important thing is “where I can put it.”

When it comes time to choose a subwoofer, there are so many different models from various manufacturers that it’s easy to get overwhelmed.
However, based on my many years of experience, I’d like to share my own selection criteria with you here.

The key is to choose based on **"where you can put it (whether you have the space)"** rather than sound quality or output specifications.
Basically, this alone will narrow down your options significantly.

My top recommendation is “under the passenger seat (or driver’s seat),” where it won’t get in the way and you can make the most of the available space. Many of the slim, powered subwoofers available from various manufacturers are designed specifically to slide into this space under the seat.

## Between Practicality and Romance (Innovations in Honda Cars)

…If things were that simple, it wouldn’t be such a hassle, but the world isn’t that forgiving.
For example, there are cars—such as certain Honda models (those with a center-tank layout where the fuel tank is located under the front seats) and the Mitsubishi i with its rear mid-ship layout—that simply don’t have any space under the front seats to accommodate a subwoofer in the first place.

To be honest, my own car is also a Honda. After peering under the seat and giving a wry smile, thinking, “There’s no way it’ll fit,” I ended up finding a spot for it under the back seat.

What’s more, a car is not only a space for hobbies but also a daily “means of transportation.” There may be times when you want to fold down the seats to load large items. That’s why we’ve put some thought into the wiring and mounting so that anyone can easily move the seats temporarily whenever they get in the way of rearranging the interior.

To be honest, I’d love nothing more than to plop a massive subwoofer box right in the trunk. But any modification that leaves no room for grocery bags could easily get me a red card and get me kicked out of the house. Finding that delicate balance between practicality and sound quality is, I suppose, one of the true joys (or the wisdom) of adult DIY.

## When the bass is just right, the world changes

Strangely enough, once the bass foundation sounds solid, even the vocals and high frequencies become much clearer. The music’s inherent energy—which had been drowned out by road noise—is restored, and the enjoyment of driving is sure to increase many times over.

So, grab a tape measure and check the dimensions under the seats and in the crevices of your beloved car, then go ahead and click to buy your favorite one for your day off.
The depths of this quagmire are still unfathomable.
