# 🎧 Basic policy for DSP sound adjustment settings

## Filter & Equalizer (EQ) settings

This setting places the front as the main focus, while the rear focuses on "thickening the sound by suppressing high frequencies (rear fill)."

### Front speaker (Pioneer TS-F1740Ⅱ)

* HPF (bass cut): 63 Hz ~ 80 Hz
* LPF (treble cut): None (all sounds in full range)
* Slope: -12 dB/oct or -18 dB/oct
* Filter type: Linkwitz-Riley
* Equalizer adjustment: By slightly raising the high frequencies (2 kHz to 4 kHz and above) that tend to get buried under your feet, the sound will rise above the dashboard.

### Rear speaker (Gathers GS-5060DL)

* HPF (bass cut): 80 Hz to 100 Hz (higher than the front to protect the rated 20W)
* LPF (treble cut): 4 kHz to 5 kHz (blocks rattling sounds from behind)
* Slope: -12 dB/oct
* Filter type: Linkwitz-Riley
* Equalizer adjustment: Slightly lower the high frequency range (8 kHz to 10 kHz) to blend in so that the sound does not get dragged into the background.

## Speaker & Woofer settings

Recommended value for a 3-way system that combines a powered subwoofer (carrozzeria TS-WX22A) to create the best balanced sound throughout the car.

| Speaker system | Filter settings (crossover) | Slope/type | Approximate output level (gain) | Role and adjustment points |
| :-- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Subwoofer (TS-WX22A) | LPF: 63 Hz to 80 Hz (does not produce sounds higher than this) | -12 dB/oct Linkwitz-Riley | Fine-tune by hand to a level that is comfortable to listen to from the driver's seat | Supports the foundation of deep bass. Connects beautifully with the same number as the front HPF. |
| Front (TS-F1740) (Medium/Treble/Vocals) | HPF: 63 Hz to 80 Hz (combined with woofer's LPF) | -12 or -18 dB/oct Linkwitz-Riley | Reference value (0 dB) Overall bass volume | Create a stage in front of your eyes with crisp, clear mid-treble that takes advantage of the inner baffle. |
| Rear (GS-5060DL) (reverberation sound/thickness) | HPF: 80 Hz LPF: 4 kHz (only the delicious part of the midrange) | -12 dB/oct Linkwitz-Riley | "-4 dB to -6 dB" lower than the front | Corrects the efficiency difference (91 dB and approximately 85 dB). It doesn't emit harsh high-pitched sounds, and only covers the faint sound inside the car. |
